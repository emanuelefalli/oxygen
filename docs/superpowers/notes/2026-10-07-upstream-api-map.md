## What this changes in the Oxygen plan

1. **Per-round commit.** `ZeppHistoryFetch` waits for `commit(roundID:durable:)` after every round. With `.keepOnDevice` the ack is `03 09` either way, so `StrapSession` can commit each round at once (`durable: false`), collect the rounds of the type, and emit `fetchCompleted(type, rounds)` at `.noData` / `.finished`. Oxygen's watermark still moves only after its own store save. One `ZeppHistoryFetch` is created per fetch type.
2. **Watermark: reuse upstream.** Use `HelioFetchPlan.advancedCursor` (last record + 1 min) instead of "newest record, inclusive", and the same clamps as `HelioFetchPlan.plan` (7-day first fetch, 30-day maximum, 5-minute future tolerance, minute floor).
3. **Key text: reuse upstream.** `StrapAuthKey.parse` delegates to `HelioKeyText.normalized` and `ZeppAuthKey(hex:)`.
4. **Strap busy comes from auth, plus an auth timeout.** Busy is any auth failure other than wrong key, or no auth reply within 10 s. The reducer needs a session timer for `authenticating` (10 s) and `preparing` (5 s per step inside the session).
5. **Notifications are switched by the session.** `StrapSession` outputs set-notify requests (`chunkedRead` and `chunkedWrite` before auth, `chunkedWrite` off after auth, `activityControl` / `activityData` on during a fetch and off after), and the connection reports when each subscription is confirmed.
6. **Link upkeep.** The session answers the `0x0015` ping and applies MTU announcements.
7. **Types the strap never fills.** Manual HR and manual stress are always empty, and `sleepSpO2` may fail to parse (the fetch machine then reports `roundFailed`). Keep fetching all 13 for raw data; treat `roundFailed` as "nothing stored this time" for that round, not as a sync failure.
8. **Field formatting.** Byte arrays become lowercase hex. Sleep stages become `start/end/kind` triples (ISO-8601 UTC, case name) joined by `;`.
9. **Test target.** No extra package products are needed (row 13).
